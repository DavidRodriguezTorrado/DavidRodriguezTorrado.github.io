This core course introduces students in the Bachelor’s Degree in Biomedical Engineering to algorithms, data structures, and concurrent programming for solving problems and developing applications and services.

The syllabus includes:

- **Programming fundamentals**, including software lifecycle, testing, and debugging.
- **Algorithm design and analysis**, including recursion, complexity, basic data structures, and searching and sorting algorithms.
- **Concurrent programming**, including threads, mutual exclusion, synchronization, monitors, and deadlock.
- **Applications and services**, including web architectures and client-side and server-side components.

The course combines lectures, problem-solving sessions, and practical laboratory work.
